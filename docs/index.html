How matching works: whole words only, so RAS won't match “parasites”. Several words are a phrase, and spaces and hyphens are interchangeable: pan RAS matches “pan-RAS”. Add * for any ending (degrad*), + to require words anywhere (KRAS + degrader) and - to exclude (KRAS -review).
